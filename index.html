Goals

    Mill Ledger

    Heartwood Mill

    Run a lumber mill deep in the forest. Keep the forest alive while you do.

    Move
    Drag the map, or WASD and arrow keys. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
    Build
    Pick a tool at the bottom left. Drag to lay conveyors and paths. R rotates a conveyor.
    Inspect
    Click staff, buildings and trees. M opens the Mill Ledger.
    Time
    Space pauses. Speed buttons sit beside the date. N turns sound on or off.

    A tribute to Lumbermill, the forest factory game by BWDev. All code and art here are new.
